# Family Assistant 0.1.0-alpha.12

Development prerelease for isolated evaluation, not a production migration release.

- Explicit parent/owner consent for a current child's reported home/away/unknown
  status, including a child without an HA account. Adults still consent only for
  themselves. A linked child can stop their own sharing.
- A separate named RU/UK/EN card review and exact retry protect the choice against
  changed child/parent identity, source replacement, stale controls and lost responses.
- Guardian identity/version is retained with the consent, not with coordinates or
  observations. Revoking that family role or identity revokes its child sharing.
- Read permission remains a separate HA gate; inactive requesting HA accounts get
  no presence projection. No Telegram messages, device actions, penalties or
  presence-based notification routing are introduced by this release.

See [Presence setup and privacy](https://github.com/Wave-is/home-assistant-family-assistant/blob/0.1.0-alpha.12/docs/presence.md).
