# Family Assistant 0.1.0-alpha.13

Development prerelease for isolated evaluation, not a production migration release.

- Report a fault, then explicitly upload and attach one private JPEG/PNG/WebP
  observation in the RU/UK/EN Maintenance card. This does not complete its task
  or send a message. Completion reports remain a separate workflow.
- Uploads remain uploader-private until attachment. Current parents, the current
  reporter identity and current assignee may view the attached photo. Identity,
  role, assignment and module changes are rechecked; unlinked HA administrators
  do not gain application access.
- Owners may explicitly delete a photo with a reason. Access ends immediately,
  crash-safe collection removes the bytes, and parent-visible history survives.
- Exact retry handles lost reserve/upload/attach/purge responses. Revocation and
  card detachment abort pending transfers and revoke browser preview URLs.
- No Telegram/AI forwarding. Original image metadata, including EXIF, may remain.
  PDF/manual-document attachments and full household migration remain pending.

See [Maintenance setup and photo privacy](https://github.com/Wave-is/home-assistant-family-assistant/blob/0.1.0-alpha.13/docs/maintenance.md).
