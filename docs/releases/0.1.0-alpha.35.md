# 0.1.0-alpha.35 — scoped spoken proposal review

Evaluation prerelease, not full-vision completion or household cutover.

- Assist shopping flow with explicit spoken confirmation/cancellation in EN/RU/UK.
  Native replies read the preview and review phrase, not an opaque internal ID.
- Saved context points only to the caller's own proposal in the same conversation.
  Exact phrases bypass the model; generic yes/no never confirms a proposal.
- New in-flight or failed turns hide older context. Concurrent late replies and
  retries cannot replace a newer conversation target. Domain expiry, identity,
  record versions and child shopping approval remain mandatory.
- Actual Core2026.8.2 authenticated binary PCM → STT → intent → Family Assistant
  acceptance, with synthetic recognizer/planner outputs. The native gate covers
  all three languages, reload between preview and confirmation, cancellation,
  stranger/session denial and a child's parent-review boundary.

Requires your own configured HA speech-to-text provider and signed-in HA account;
free-form planning also needs your own configured model. No implicit speaker
recognition, owner identity for anonymous satellites, Telegram voice-note support,
physical microphone/TTS accuracy claim or household deployment.
