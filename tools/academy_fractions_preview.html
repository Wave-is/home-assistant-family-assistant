Fraction Lab

Power a space station by mastering equal parts. About 15–20 minutes.

01 · Explore

A fraction describes equal parts of a whole. The numerator counts selected parts; the denominator counts all equal parts.

Split every piece in two: both the numerator and denominator double, but the amount stays the same.

02 · Add and apply

To add fractions, use equal-sized parts: 1/4 + 1/2 = 1/4 + 2/4 = 3/4. To find 3/4 of 20 cells, divide 20 by 4, then multiply by 3.

Ukrainian glossary: fraction — дріб; numerator — чисельник; denominator — знаменник; equivalent — рівнозначний.

Fun fact

Ancient Egyptians often expressed quantities as sums of unit fractions, such as 1/2 + 1/3.
